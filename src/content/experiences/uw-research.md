I worked with Professor Damiano Torre at UW’s Software Engineering for Secure AI-based Systems Research Lab on federated learning for intrusion detection on resource-constrained devices.

I reviewed 20+ FL-IDS papers and found a validation gap that led to an architecture proposal.

I designed a three-layer hierarchical FL architecture using entropy-weighted aggregation and selective homomorphic encryption. I also built a preprocessing pipeline for four public IDS datasets.

[IDS Multi-Dataset Preprocessing Pipeline on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code/daniilnahliuk1/ids-multi-dataset-preprocessing-pipeline)
